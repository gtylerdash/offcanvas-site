the artist

Founded by artist Riley Morrison

Off Canvas creates hand-painted, one-of-one works of art on unexpected forms — from surfboards and sports pieces to traditional canvas and whatever comes next.

Every piece starts by hand. Some works are born from Riley's own inspiration; others are commissioned and designed around the people, places, colors and stories that matter to their owners.

Each finished work is signed by Riley and marked 1/1 — a promise that it is, and always will be, the only one.
